Instructions in case of fire

[I.] In case of fire, activate the fire alarm and call the Fire Services Department.
[II.] If a fire occurs in a building, do not use lifts to evacuate.
[III.] If you see a fire in your escape route, stay in a safe place with the door closed.
[IV.] You should extinguish the fire with available equipment only if it is safe to do so.
[V.] You should not re-enter the workplace unless it is certified safe to do so.

Adapted from 'Guidance Notes on Fire Safety at Workplaces' published by the Labour Department

🎯 Language Focus

1. Understanding rules and regulations

Usage

Rules and regulations are sets of principles that people must follow for a particular activity. In guides and COPs, rules and regulations are given to practitioners in the specific industry to ensure that they can carry out their work properly.

Strength of Obligation Scale

In safety instructions, different words show different levels of obligation (how strongly you must follow the rule):

may should must/shall imperative

WEAKEST ←―――――――――――――――――――→ STRONGEST
(suggestion)     (expected)     (required by law)     (direct command)

Feature

We can identify rules and regulations with the following language features:

Imperative:
Activate the fire alarm and call the Fire Services Department.
Should:
You should extinguish the fire with available equipment
Shall:
Employers or managers shall give briefing sessions and written instructions on fire safety to all newly appointed employees before they start work.
Must:
All employers must ensure that all means of escape are unobstructed and can be reached safely at all times.

2. Understanding conditions

Usage

In guides and COPs, conditional sentences are usually used to talk about rules and regulations with conditions.

Feature

We can identify conditions with the following language features:

If:
If a fire occurs in a building, do not use lifts to evacuate.
In case (of):
In case of fire, activate the fire alarm and call the Fire Services Department.
Unless:
You should not re-enter the workplace unless it is certified safe to do so
Only if:
You should extinguish the fire only if it is safe to do so.

3. Recognising synonyms (similar words)

Usage

Synonyms are different words that have similar or the same meanings. Learning synonyms helps you:

  • Understand the text better
  • Match information in questions with words in the text
  • Expand your vocabulary

Example in Text 1

extinguish ⟷ put out
certify ⟷ confirm
activate ⟷ switch on
route ⟷ path
