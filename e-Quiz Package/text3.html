General Guide on Occupational Safety and Health

🔧 Excerpt (a) - Tool Safety

Many workplace accidents occurred because of selecting a wrong tool, misuse of tools and accessories or lack of proper maintenance of tools. Therefore, workers should select a tool suitable for the specific job and follow the safe instructions to use and maintain it. Employers or responsible persons should make sure that workers have received training in the safe operating procedures and skills for the tools concerned.

🪜 Excerpt (b) - Ladder and Height Safety

Ladders should be used for access to and egress from high places only. It is unsafe for workers to work on a ladder as they may fall from it during work. Many serious injuries or even fatalities are due to using ladders inappropriately. A working platform should be used instead for common work above the ground. To ensure safety, a risk assessment should be conducted before working at height by a competent person to eliminate or minimise the fall hazards. Fall prevention equipment should also be provided as far as practicable to safeguard workers working at height.

⚠️ Excerpt (c) - Confined Space Safety

Workers are exposed to toxic gases when working in confined spaces such as tunnels and wells. Other potential hazards in confined spaces include suffocation, fire, rush of sewage and caving in of sand. To protect themselves, workers shall use suitable types of breathing apparatus or gas mask during work. As the work environment may change continuously, which increases the risk of the work, a competent person shall recommend the use of appropriate air monitoring equipment. Also, only certified workers shall be allowed to enter the confined spaces or work inside there.

Adapted from 'Occupational Safety and Health Management in Renovation and Maintenance Works for the Property Management Industry' published by the Labour Department

🎯 Language Focus

1. 'Because of' & 'because'

Usage

We can use 'because of' or 'because' to state the reason for something.

Form & Example

'Because of' is followed by a noun or noun phrase (NP)

E.g. Workers may suffer from illness because of heat or cold stress.

'Because' is followed by a clause (SVO structure)

E.g. Workers usually get hurt because they use a machine inappropriately.

2. 'Due to' & 'owing to'

Usage

We can also use 'due to' and 'owing to' to introduce the reason for something. They are commonly used in formal documents such as guides and COP.

Form & Example

Both of them are followed by a noun or noun phrase (NP)

E.g. A number of fall accidents are due to the lack of suitable fall protection.

E.g. Owing to the nature of work, some workers have to carry out work on live conductors.
