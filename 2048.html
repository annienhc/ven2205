What are Hyponyms and Hypernyms?

Hyponym: A more specific word (e.g., "smartphone" is a hyponym of "gadget").
Hypernym: A more general word (e.g., "technology" is a hypernym of "electronics").
In this game, merging identical terms creates their hypernym (broader category)!
