📖 How to Play

▼

🎮 Solo Mode Objective

Group vocabulary cards into their correct word families. Each family shares a common root word and related meanings. Complete all cards before running out of lives!

⚔️ VS Mode Objective

Compete against a friend in split-screen! Take turns placing cards correctly. Earn points for accuracy and speed. Player with the most points when all cards are placed wins!

🎯 Solo Mode Controls

  • Click the deck to draw a card
  • Drag the card to the correct family zone
  • Group all words with the same root together
  • You have 5 hearts - don't waste them!

👥 VS Mode Rules

  • Players take turns placing cards
  • 30 seconds per turn or you lose your turn
  • Correct placement: +10 points
  • Place within 3 seconds: +5 bonus points!
  • Wrong placement: -5 points, skip turn

❤️ Lives System (Solo)

You have 5 hearts in solo mode. Each incorrect placement loses one heart. Place cards carefully! Run out of hearts and the game ends.

⏱️ Timer & Scoring

Solo mode: Race against the clock for better scores. VS mode: 30-second turn limit with speed bonuses for quick placements!
